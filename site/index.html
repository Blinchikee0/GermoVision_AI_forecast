Mutation heat along sequence

Vertical bars = per-position impact score (0…1). Red bars sit near known escape hotspots; grey band shows hotspot regions of the reference.

Impact distribution

—

Stacked bars: how many mutations fall into each impact bin (0.0-0.9). Red = high (≥0.6), amber = medium, black = low. A heavy right tail is a warning sign.

Hotspot proximity

—

Distance in residues from each mutation to the nearest known escape hotspot. First bar (0-4 residues) is the danger zone.

Physicochemical shift

Combined shift in hydrophobicity, charge and residue mass caused by each substitution. Values > 0.5 usually break local structure.

Cumulative impact along sequence

Running sum of mutation impact as we walk from residue 1 to the C-terminus. Steep segments = high-density mutation clusters.

Amino-acid composition

Percent share of each amino acid in the submitted sequence. Deviations from a reference protein hint at codon-usage bias or truncation.

Hydrophobicity profile

Kyte-Doolittle rolling mean (window = 9). Peaks are transmembrane / hydrophobic core segments — mutations here often disrupt folding.

Local entropy (bits)

Shannon entropy of a 21-residue window. Low values = biased / repetitive stretches; high values = flexible / disordered regions.

Mutation type split

Substitution vs insertion vs deletion. Deletions are structurally disruptive and often drive immune escape.

Position × impact scatter

Each dot is one mutation. Vertical clusters at hotspot positions signal a coordinated escape event, not random drift.

Regional mutation load

Reference sequence split into 10 equal windows. Bars show how many mutations fall in each window — reveals localised vs distributed pressure.

Attached image · cross-reference

—

Byte-intensity histogram (16 bins). Used only as a coarse quality & cross-reference signal; the ML call remains driven by the sequence.
